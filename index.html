Supply programs

Structured supply, not retail shelves

We don't sell by the piece. We allocate supply through programs designed for operational kitchens that need consistency above all else.

PROGRAM 01

Volume Rice Supply

Scheduled weekly or bi-weekly rice allocation in commercial sacks. Consistent grade, consistent delivery. Built for kitchens that go through 5+ sacks per week.

PROGRAM 02

Poultry & Egg Allocation

Farm-fresh eggs graded by exact weight in grams — not vague S/M/L labels. Whole chicken available fresh or frozen. Allocated per account, not sold off a shelf.

PROGRAM 03

Bulk Cooking Oil Distribution

Commercial-grade cooking oil in institutional volumes. Scheduled delivery ensures your kitchen never runs dry mid-service.

PROGRAM 04

Essential Kitchen Staples

A tailored combination of all supply categories — rice, eggs, poultry, oil, and staples — bundled into a single weekly delivery schedule customized to your kitchen's consumption pattern. One account. One delivery. Everything documented.

From Amadeo, for Amadeo — and beyond

Must Have Corner started as a family-run supply operation in Amadeo, Cavite. We saw how institutions — rehab centers, retreat houses, small schools — struggled with inconsistent suppliers, missing invoices, and last-minute shortages that disrupted their kitchens.

So we built something different. Not a grocery store. Not a market stall. A structured supply system with account-based ordering, documented deliveries, and credit terms that respect how real businesses operate.

Today, we serve kitchens across South Cavite that feed dozens to hundreds of people daily. Our clients stay because the supply shows up on time, the invoice arrives in their inbox, and they never have to chase us for records.

We don't compete on who's cheapest. We compete on who's most reliable.
